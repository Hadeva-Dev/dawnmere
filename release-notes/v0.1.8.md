# Dawnmere 0.1.8

Chinese and Japanese recall. Update with `dawnmere update`, or run the install line again.

## Chinese and Japanese

- Your apps' prompts now get what you saved in Chinese or Japanese when you ask about it in the same words. Before, a
  question written without spaces was never searched.
- Keyword search reads Chinese and Japanese. Memories you saved before this update are indexed the first time it runs.
- A memory reaches a Chinese or Japanese prompt only when it shares enough of the question's words. A question asked
  in different words than you saved is often missed; your app can still find it by searching.
- A prompt that needs no memory, in any language, still gets none.
- With a model set up, a prompt written mostly in Chinese or Japanese is also searched in English, so it can find what
  you saved in English. That is one small model call per such prompt, counted in the 200 a day, sent to your own
  provider. An English prompt that only names someone in Chinese or Japanese costs nothing.
- Notes about your health, family, money and legal matters in Chinese and Japanese are kept personal in more cases,
  including when you leave out "I" ("母が入院している").
- When an app saves a health or money note, the reply no longer says every app can see it. Apps set to work only never
  see those.
- Notes an app saves on its own still reach other apps' prompts only after you confirm them, in every language.

## Privacy

- The privacy list now says what the weekly tidy-up sends: once a week, and once soon after setup, memories that say nearly the same thing, memories that read as out of date and dated status notes, each with a few related memories, go to your model to be merged, rewritten or retired. They count toward the 200 calls a day; with no model, none of this is sent.
