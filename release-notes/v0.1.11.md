# Dawnmere 0.1.11

Search made for Chinese and Japanese. Update with `dawnmere update`, or run the install line again.

## Chinese and Japanese

- Questions in Chinese or Japanese are now searched with two models made for many languages, on your Mac. A note asked
  about in its own words is found more reliably, and a Chinese or Japanese question can find a note you saved in
  English, even without a model set up.
- A question worded differently from your note is still often missed; your app can still search for it.
- The two models (about 270 MB) are not part of setup. The first time you save or ask something in Chinese or
  Japanese, Dawnmere downloads them in the background from this repo's models release and checks every file. Until
  then, Chinese and Japanese work as before. `dawnmere doctor` says whether they are there, and
  `dawnmere models --get embed-ml rerank-ml` downloads them now.
- English is unchanged: English questions never use these models.
- A prompt that needs no memory, in any language, still gets none, and notes an app saves on its own still wait for
  your confirm.
