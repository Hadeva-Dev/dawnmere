# Dawnmere 0.1.14

Update with `dawnmere update`, or run the install line again. This release updates the engine to 0.1.12; the CLI, the plugin and the connector are unchanged apart from their version.

- **Someone else's words no longer overwrite your own note.** Before, a pasted email, a web page or an app's save that said nearly the same thing with a different value (another account number, say) replaced your note, and the same words from a less trusted source could stop your note reaching your prompts. Now your note keeps its words. The other value is shown only directly under your note, tagged `[another version; not confirmed]`, or not at all. Typing "remember that ..." still replaces a note at once.
- **An app's forget no longer takes your own copies of a note.** Your typed copies and a Claude Code memory file holding your words stay, and the reply names them. Your own forget takes them all, as before.
- **Notes saved before Dawnmere recorded who wrote them** (from its earliest versions) are treated as your own.
- **A forget made while another app was reading the memory** could leave the forgotten words in the memory file until a later forget. The clean-up is now checked and retried until it completes.
